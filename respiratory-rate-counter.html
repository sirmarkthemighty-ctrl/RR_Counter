Keyboard: Space or Enter = breath · Backspace = undo · Esc = reset. A tap more than 60 s after the last one starts a new count.

Bars appear after the second tap: one bar per breath, height = 60 ÷ that interval. The dashed line is the average.

    Children <5 y — WHO IMCI fast breathing [2]

    AgeFast breathing if
    Under 2 months≥60 /min
    2–12 months≥50 /min
    12 months – 5 years≥40 /min

    5–15 years: no threshold in this app's sources, so the score shows N/A. The interval-variation figure is shown as a number only; the app does not label it regular or irregular.

    • Each tap marks the start of a breath. N taps give N − 1 intervals.
    • Rate = 60 × (number of intervals) ÷ (seconds from first to last tap). This is the same as 60 ÷ the mean interval.
    • Last 5 breaths uses the last 5 intervals only, so it follows a change in rate faster.
    • Interval variation = standard deviation ÷ mean of the intervals (shown from 3 intervals on).
    • The bar fills over 60 s from the first tap. WHO IMCI asks for breaths to be counted over one full minute in children [2]; a short count from a few taps can be off by several breaths per minute.
    • Tap the same point of each breath (e.g. start of chest rise), and watch without telling the patient, as people change their breathing when they know it is being counted.
    1. Royal College of Physicians. National Early Warning Score (NEWS) 2: Standardising the assessment of acute-illness severity in the NHS. London: RCP, 2017. rcp.ac.uk
    2. World Health Organization. Integrated Management of Childhood Illness (IMCI) chart booklet. Geneva: WHO, 2014. who.int
